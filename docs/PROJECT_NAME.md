# 为什么叫团子小屋 / Why Dango House?

**公开名称：团子小屋 · Dango House**  
**副标题：AI 娘乐园 · A tiny home for your AI companions**  
**仓库名：`dango-house`**

我们参考了 [AIRI](https://github.com/moeru-ai/airi) 的角色品牌名、[CrossPet](https://github.com/lokicorvus/crosspet) 的简短产品名，以及 [Shimeji Desktop](https://github.com/ikum14/shimeji-desktop) 的桌宠品类命名方式（2026-10-30 调研）。这里的取舍是使用容易记住的独立名字，再以副标题解释用途。  
Naming research on 2026-10-30 considered AIRI's character branding, CrossPet's compact product name, and Shimeji Desktop's category-based name. We chose a memorable identity with a descriptive subtitle.

“团子”呼应 Q 版角色和现有猫团子图标；“小屋”对应共居、布置和陪伴。比起把“后宫”直接写进主名，它也适合朋友、宠物和亲密陪伴等不同关系玩法；争宠、撒娇和偏爱仍然是介绍里的特色。  
“Dango” matches the chibi cast and existing mascot; “House” suggests shared living, decoration, and company. It accommodates friendship, pet-like companionship, and affectionate roleplay, while playful jealousy stays part of the experience.

名称延续现有应用，避免让下载用户以为是另一个产品；并不表示对商标或全球重名情况做过全面检索。参考项目与本项目无合作或背书关系。  
Keeping the existing app name avoids confusing users. This is not an exhaustive trademark or global name-clearance search. Referenced projects are independent and do not endorse this one.
