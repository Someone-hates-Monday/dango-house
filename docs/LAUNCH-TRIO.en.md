# Trio launch plan — Dango House

[中文完整分镜与执行方案](LAUNCH-TRIO.zh-CN.md)

2026-11-04. Launch after GPT and Claude are polished and DeepSeek/Claude/GPT interactions and CG pass art/runtime acceptance. Planning only: no posting, spending or resumed generation.

Positioning: **Bring three AI companions home to your desktop. Be their favorite—or enjoy their chemistry.** Lead with playful user-directed affection and character-to-character romance, then show practical integration. Other residents remain optional works in progress.

Original 60-second storyboard: 0–3s select Claude from the Dock; 3–9s distinct rival reactions; 9–16s Claude invites DeepSeek closer; 16–23s an actual in-app head-bubble CG; 23–30s one user-directed caring gesture; 30–38s touch/feed/quiet Dock life; 38–45s a verified MCP/bridge request and visible response; 45–54s real backend/persona settings, cast size, address and desktop modes; 54–60s trio Dock signature, product name and one download CTA.

Use fast 1–2s feature cuts, readable 2–3s reactions and a roughly 4s CG highlight. Proposed licensed soundtrack: 120–132 BPM. Never speed up character animation just to hit beats. Export Chinese and English captions separately. Actual reference-film timing has not been inspected: the Harness-inspired pacing is a proposal, not a shot-by-shot reconstruction.

References: [DeepSeek Harness](https://www.deepseek.com/zh/harness/) for composable capabilities, [ChatVRM](https://github.com/pixiv/ChatVRM) for character-first presentation, [AutoGPT](https://github.com/Significant-Gravitas/AutoGPT) for communicating a goal before architecture. These are presentation references, not proven explanations of virality.

Deliverables: 60s landscape launch film; 15s affection hook; 20s character-romance cut; 30s developer/integration cut; 2–3min installation tutorial; bilingual README with Dock animation and a direct tested-release download.

Launch gates: complete and consistent GPT/Claude interactions; reproducible trio scenes; one accepted pair CG and one user-POV CG; actual host integration; honest single-backend persona labels; a clean-profile tested Windows package; attribution and music/art permissions. If a gate is unmet, reduce advertised scope rather than fake the shot. Record original real-time captures, build version, trigger steps and source labels. Do not advertise unimplemented preference switches, seven autonomous models or universal one-click host support.

Relative rollout: T−7 to −3 invited testing; T−2 short teaser; T0 main film + same-version release + tutorial; T+1 to +3 romance/POV/integration cuts; T+4 to +7 authorized user clips, fixes and contribution invitations. No automated promotion in other people's comment sections.

Track available retention, clicks, downloads and voluntary successful-start reports; unknown metrics stay unknown. Test affection versus romance hooks separately without claiming causal results from one organic post. Fix onboarding when downloads fail to become successful play sessions. Generation remains paused until the user resumes it.
