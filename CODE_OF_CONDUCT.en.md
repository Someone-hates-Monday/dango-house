# Code of Conduct

**Language / 语言 / 言語:** [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md) · [English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.en.md) · [日本語](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ja.md)

Everyone interested in desktop pets, anime, animation, or open source is welcome. Respect people regardless of experience, language, identity, or background.

## Please do

- Discuss concrete issues, disagree kindly, and help newcomers.
- Respect creators, attribution, and permissions; distinguish fan interpretations from official material.
- Redact keys, private chats, and personal information before sharing screenshots.
- Keep public content suitable for general audiences; keep fictional rivalry playful.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, hate speech, personal attacks, threats, stalking, doxxing, nonconsensual disclosure, sexual harassment or explicit content, and spam. Do not attack people over character, model, or pairing preferences.

## Scope & enforcement

Applies to issues, PRs, discussions, and public spaces representing the project. Maintainers may warn, remove content, restrict participation, or ban users. Use GitHub reporting tools or email **2872397866@qq.com**. Do not post sensitive evidence in public issues.
