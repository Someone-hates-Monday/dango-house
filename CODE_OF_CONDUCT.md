# 小屋社区约定 / Code of Conduct

欢迎喜欢桌宠、二次元、动画和开源的每个人。无论经验、语言、身份和背景，请让彼此感到被尊重。  
Everyone interested in desktop pets, anime, animation, or open source is welcome. Respect people regardless of experience, language, identity, or background.

## 请这样相处 / Please do

- 讨论具体问题，友善表达分歧，给新人解释上下文。Discuss concrete issues, disagree kindly, and help newcomers.
- 尊重创作者、署名和授权，区分同人解读与官方设定。Respect creators, attribution, and permissions; distinguish fan interpretations from official material.
- 分享截图前移除密钥、私人对话和个人信息。Redact keys, private chats, and personal information.
- 公共讨论和素材保持全年龄友好，角色争宠保持轻松。Keep public content suitable for general audiences and fictional rivalry playful.

## 不接受的行为 / Unacceptable behavior

骚扰、歧视、仇恨言论、人身攻击、威胁、跟踪、人肉搜索、未经同意披露隐私、性骚扰或露骨内容、广告刷屏。不要攻击他人的角色、模型或配对偏好。  
Harassment, discrimination, hate speech, personal attacks, threats, stalking, doxxing, nonconsensual disclosure, sexual harassment or explicit content, and spam are not acceptable. Do not attack people over character, model, or pairing preferences.

## 范围与处理 / Scope & enforcement

适用于 Issue、PR、Discussions 和代表项目参与的公共交流。维护者可提醒、编辑/移除内容、限制互动或封禁；严重或重复行为可直接限制。处理时说明原因并保护举报者隐私。  
Applies to issues, PRs, discussions, and public spaces where people represent the project. Maintainers may warn, remove content, restrict participation, or ban users; severe or repeated violations may warrant immediate restriction. Explain decisions and protect reporters' privacy.

Use GitHub's reporting tools or privately email **2872397866@qq.com**. Do not post sensitive evidence in public issues. Reports will be reviewed; no fixed response time is currently guaranteed.
